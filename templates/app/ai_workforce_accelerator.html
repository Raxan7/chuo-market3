Program journey

Eight weeks from AI awareness to applied capability.

The sequence starts with foundations and progressively moves toward role-specific application, workflow design, governance and capstone delivery.

Week 1

AI-Ready Professional

Models, strengths, limits, human oversight and task selection.

Week 2

Prompting & Problem Structuring

Turn vague requests into repeatable, high-quality instructions.

Week 3

Research & Knowledge Work

Evidence, documents, synthesis, verification and executive briefs.

Week 4

Data & Decision Support

Spreadsheets, patterns, anomalies, scenarios and interpretation.

Week 5

Communication & Productivity

Meetings, reports, proposals, presentations and stakeholder communication.

Week 6

Automation & Workflows

Triggers, structured workflows, no-code concepts, agents and controls.

Week 7

Role Specialization

Deepen application through department-specific professional challenges.

Week 8

Responsible AI & Capstone

Governance, final practical, capstone presentation and certification.

Practical by design

Employees demonstrate the skill.

The learning model uses realistic workplace tasks: research briefs, long documents, data analysis, meetings, reports, workflows, risk reviews and capstones.

  • 16+ common practical labs plus role-specific challenges.
  • Rubric-based scoring with feedback and resubmission.
  • Capstone projects connected to a real or realistic organizational workflow.
  • Final practical assessment designed to test transfer, judgement and safety.
Evidence management can inspect.Progress, capability, practical performance and projects can be surfaced as outcomes.
